## What I solve

I design interfaces that make a technical proposition understandable and connect
acquisition, product and operations. Design reduces friction and makes value
visible.

Mobilec project status refers to my employment there, which has ended;
it does not imply that I currently maintain those systems.

## Selected implementations

### Mobilec lead capture landing page · production

A landing page built for Mobilec that replaced reliance on an external platform.
It connects the commercial proposition with a channel where the assistant can
continue the interaction and help prepare a quote.

### Jit De Flou · public website and services

The [Jit De Flou website](https://jitdeflou.com) is operational.
I built its interface with Tailwind CSS and designed the journey from service
offering to virtual agent and continued human support. Product status is
documented in
[Full-stack products](./full-stack-products.md).

### Radar Inmobiliario · historical

A multichannel funnel for an event streamed on YouTube: acquisition through
social media, CRM, email, WhatsApp, incremental automation, a conversational
agent and operation of the broadcast.

### Quinto Elemento · historical

Technical implementation of a landing page designed by another professional:
AWS infrastructure, self-hosted WordPress and functional component integration.
I do not claim authorship of the visual design.

## Decisions and trade-offs

- Design the full journey, beyond an isolated landing page.
- Connect commercial communication with an actionable next step.
- Automate while retaining the option of human support.
- Clearly distinguish my contribution within collaborative work.
